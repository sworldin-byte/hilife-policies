Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) Prevention Policy

HiLife's Commitment to Child Safety

Last Updated: August 2025

1. Introduction and Purpose

HiLife is firmly committed to creating a safe environment for all users, with special emphasis on protecting children and minors from sexual abuse and exploitation. This policy outlines our zero-tolerance stance against Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) and the comprehensive measures we implement to prevent, detect, and respond to such content and behavior on our platform.

Zero Tolerance Policy: HiLife maintains an absolute zero-tolerance policy towards any form of child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, exploitation, or any content or behavior that sexualizes, endangers, or harms minors in any way.

2. Scope and Definitions

This policy applies to all users, content, and interactions on the HiLife platform. For the purposes of this policy:

  • Child/Minor: Any person under the age of 18 years.
  • CSAM (Child Sexual Abuse Material): Any visual depiction (image, video, or digital content including AI-generated) of sexually explicit conduct involving a minor.
  • Grooming: Actions deliberately undertaken with the aim of befriending and establishing an emotional connection with a minor to lower inhibitions in preparation for sexual abuse or exploitation.
  • Exploitation: Any actual or attempted abuse of a position of vulnerability, differential power, or trust for sexual purposes, including profiting monetarily, socially, or politically from sexual exploitation of a minor.
  • Sexualization of Minors: Any content that depicts minors in a sexual manner, even if it does not meet the legal definition of CSAM.

3. Prohibited Content and Behavior

The following is strictly prohibited on HiLife:

  1. Sharing, uploading, distributing, or soliciting CSAM in any form, including AI-generated or digitally manipulated content.
  2. Grooming or attempting to groom minors for sexual purposes.
  3. Soliciting sexual content from minors or sending sexually explicit material to minors.
  4. Any content that sexualizes minors, including fictional depictions.
  5. Sharing or requesting personal information of minors for exploitative purposes.
  6. Facilitating, promoting, or glorifying child sexual abuse or exploitation.
  7. Using the platform to arrange real-world sexual contact with minors.
  8. Sextortion or blackmail involving minors.
  9. Trafficking of minors for sexual purposes.
  10. Any attempt to circumvent our child safety measures.

4. Prevention Measures

4.1 Technical Safeguards

  • Implementation of hash-matching technology (PhotoDNA and similar) to detect known CSAM.
  • AI and machine learning-based content moderation systems trained to detect potentially harmful content involving minors.
  • Automated detection of grooming language patterns and suspicious behavioral indicators.
  • Age verification mechanisms during account registration.
  • Restrictions on direct messaging between adult users and identified minor accounts.

4.2 Human Moderation

  • Dedicated trust and safety team trained in identifying CSAE-related content and behavior.
  • Regular review of flagged content by trained moderators.
  • Specialized training for moderation staff on trauma-informed approaches and emerging exploitation tactics.

4.3 User Education

  • Clear community guidelines that explicitly prohibit CSAE-related content.
  • Safety resources and educational content for users, especially younger users.
  • Easy-to-find reporting mechanisms for suspicious behavior.

5. Detection and Reporting

5.1 Internal Detection

HiLife employs multiple layers of detection:

  • Automated scanning of uploaded content against known CSAM databases.
  • Behavioral analysis systems to identify patterns consistent with grooming or exploitation.
  • Proactive monitoring of high-risk interaction patterns.
  • Regular audits of platform content and user reports.

5.2 User Reporting

We provide accessible reporting tools that allow any user to report:

  • Suspected CSAM content.
  • Suspicious behavior toward minors.
  • Grooming attempts.
  • Any content or interaction that may endanger a child.

5.3 External Reporting

HiLife reports identified CSAM and exploitation to:

  • The National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) via CyberTipline.
  • Relevant local law enforcement authorities.
  • The Internet Watch Foundation (IWF) and other applicable organizations.
  • Indian authorities including NCRB (National Crime Records Bureau) and local cyber crime cells as applicable under Indian law.

6. Response and Enforcement

6.1 Immediate Actions

Upon detection or report of CSAE content or behavior, HiLife will:

  1. Immediately remove the offending content.
  2. Suspend the offending account pending investigation.
  3. Preserve all relevant evidence for law enforcement.
  4. Report to NCMEC and relevant authorities within 24 hours.
  5. Notify law enforcement if there is an imminent threat to a child's safety.

6.2 Account Actions

  • Permanent ban of accounts found to be involved in CSAE.
  • Prohibition of new account creation by banned users.
  • Removal of all content associated with the offending account.

6.3 Cooperation with Authorities

HiLife fully cooperates with law enforcement investigations related to CSAE, including:

  • Providing requested data in accordance with legal requirements.
  • Preserving evidence upon lawful request.
  • Responding promptly to emergency disclosure requests involving child safety.

7. Data Retention and Evidence Preservation

For CSAE-related incidents:

  • Reported content is preserved securely for law enforcement purposes and is not accessible to general staff.
  • Account data and activity logs related to CSAE cases are retained as required by applicable law.
  • Evidence is stored in encrypted, access-controlled systems with strict chain-of-custody protocols.
  • Data retention complies with all applicable legal requirements including the Information Technology Act, 2000 (India) and POCSO Act, 2012.

8. Staff Training and Accountability

  • All employees undergo mandatory training on CSAE identification and reporting.
  • Trust and safety team members receive specialized advanced training.
  • Regular refresher training to stay current with evolving threats and tactics.
  • Mental health support for staff members who handle CSAE-related content.
  • Clear internal accountability structures for child safety compliance.

9. Collaboration and Partnerships

HiLife actively collaborates with:

  • NCMEC (National Center for Missing & Exploited Children)
  • IWF (Internet Watch Foundation)
  • INHOPE network of hotlines
  • Tech Coalition (voluntary principles to counter online CSEA)
  • Local NGOs working on child protection in India
  • Law enforcement agencies at national and international levels

10. Transparency and Accountability

  • Regular publication of transparency reports detailing CSAE enforcement actions.
  • Periodic independent audits of our child safety systems and processes.
  • Engagement with child safety experts and advocacy groups for policy review.
  • Continuous improvement of our detection and prevention capabilities.

11. Legal Compliance

This policy complies with applicable laws and regulations including but not limited to:

  • The Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act, 2012 (India)
  • Information Technology Act, 2000 and IT (Intermediary Guidelines) Rules, 2021 (India)
  • United States PROTECT Act
  • EU Directive on combating sexual abuse of children
  • Google Play Store Developer Program Policies on Child Safety
  • Apple App Store Guidelines on child safety

12. Contact and Reporting

Report CSAE Content or Behavior

If you encounter any content or behavior on HiLife that may involve the sexual abuse or exploitation of a child, please report it immediately:

  • In-App: Use the "Report" button on any content or profile.
  • Email: safety@hilife-app.com
  • Emergency: If a child is in immediate danger, please contact local law enforcement immediately.

Indian Helplines:

  • Childline India: 1098
  • Cyber Crime Helpline: 1930
  • National Commission for Protection of Child Rights (NCPCR): 011-23478200

13. Policy Updates

This policy is reviewed and updated regularly to reflect changes in law, technology, and best practices in child safety. Users will be notified of significant changes. We are committed to continuously strengthening our protections for children on our platform.

Our Commitment: HiLife is dedicated to being a safe platform for all users. We continuously invest in technology, people, and processes to protect children from abuse and exploitation. Every member of our team shares this responsibility.
